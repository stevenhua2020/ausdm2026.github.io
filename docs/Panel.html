Education: B.S., M.Eng. and Ph.D. in Computer Science, Nanjing University, China

Research Interests: AI interpretability; faithfulness of LLM reasoning; robustness and scalability of data analytics systems

Biography: Dr Chen Wang is a Principal Research Scientist at CSIRO, where he leads the Applied AI Systems research team. He leads the development of technologies for large-scale data analytics across scientific domains including radio astronomy, health and agriculture. He has published more than 90 papers and joined CSIRO in 2008, after roles as a research fellow at the University of Sydney, a software engineer at Amicas Inc. (USA) and a postdoctoral researcher at the National University of Singapore.

Key Contributions:

  • Developed well-cited algorithms for graph neural network interpretability and defence
  • Led projects applying machine learning to detect rare signals in data from large radio telescopes, resulting in the discovery of multiple pulsars and several fast radio burst candidates
  • Led the design and development of MAGDA++, a data-centric agentic AI platform for robust decision-making, used to improve plant-based protein development in agriculture and to predict finance sustainability in the government sector
