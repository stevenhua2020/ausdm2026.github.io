The 24th Australasian Data Science and Machine Learning Conference (AusDM'26)

Sydney, Australia | 02-04 December 2026

Partner with AusDM’26

The 24th Australasian Data Science and Machine Learning Conference (AusDM’26) will bring together researchers, practitioners, students, and industry leaders for three days of exchange, collaboration, and discovery in Sydney. Since 2002, AusDM has provided a forum for research papers, industry case studies, student projects, workshops, tutorials, and emerging developments in data science and machine learning.

AusDM’26 will explore the theme “Entering a New World Driven by Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence” reflecting the growing impact of intelligent technologies across industries. Sponsorship provides an opportunity for organisations to support the community while building visibility, attracting talent, sharing expertise, and developing research and industry partnerships.

Why support AusDM’26?

A partnership with AusDM’26 can help your organisation engage with a focused audience working across data analytics, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and applied data science. Sponsors may use the conference to communicate their work, meet potential employees and collaborators, showcase products or services, and contribute to the development of the next generation of researchers and practitioners. For sponsorship from universities, research institutes, professional associations, and community organisations, the contribution may be directed toward student participation, accessibility, or community-building activities, subject to agreement with the organisers.

Engagement opportunities

AusDM’26 welcomes practical, research-led, and community-focused partnerships. Depending on the level of support and the final program, sponsors may be invited to contribute through an approved industry presentation, a research or technology demonstration, a student mentoring session, a recruitment briefing, or a short lightning talk. These opportunities are intended to create useful conversations rather than replace the technical and scholarly focus of the conference.

All logos, advertisements, profiles, videos, and other promotional material must be supplied by the sponsor by the deadlines set by the organisers and remain subject to organiser approval. Sponsorship does not guarantee acceptance of a paper, workshop, tutorial, or other scholarly submission.

Partner with us

To discuss a sponsorship package or create a tailored partnership, please contact the AusDM’26 Sponsorship Chair at ausdm26@googlegroups.com
