Terms and Conditions

  • Full registration includes all sessions scheduled on Days 1, 2, and 3, social networking sessions, and the conference banquet.
  • Single Day registration includes all sessions scheduled on that particular day but does not include the conference banquet. To attend the conference banquet, please purchase a separate ticket.
  • The Early Bird Registration period will close on Friday, 25 September 2026 at 11:59 pm (Sydney time, AEST) .
  • Each accepted paper requires at least one Non-Student Full Registration to be completed by the end of the Early Bird Registration period for the paper to be included in the conference proceedings. If you have more than one accepted paper, each paper requires a separate Non-Student Full Registration.
  • All fees are in Australian dollars (AUD). An additional 10% Australian GST and platform surcharges will apply.
  • To request an invitation letter, please contact ausdm26@gmail.com.
  • All registrations are non-refundable.
